## 1. Executive summary and purpose

**Subject:** area scanning field guide for the 3D reconstruction and mapping protocol.

This manual gives the standardized procedure to do a high-density 360 spatial scan of a target area with a DJI 360 camera. The perimeter-and-grid movement pattern gives the maximum visual overlap for 3D reconstruction and mapping.

## 2. Hardware requirements and pre-recording setup

### Essential hardware checklist

- **DJI 360 camera:** the primary capture device for panoramic spherical imagery.
- **Monopod:** a rigid, stable mount.
- **Power and memory:** spare batteries for extended operations, and a minimum of **128 GB memory storage**.
- **Mobile control device:** a smartphone that runs the DJI MIMO app, for remote monitoring and capture control.

![DJI 360 camera on a monopod](11-dji-camera.png)

### Camera configuration setup

1. **Power on:** press the **Power** button once to turn on the camera.
2. **Video settings:** set the recording resolution to **5.7K at 30 FPS**.
3. **App pair (optional):** enable Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on your mobile device to connect to the DJI MIMO app for remote start and stop.
4. **Scanning height:** hold the camera at **1.6 meters (5 feet 3 inches)** above ground level.

![Scanning height of 1.6 m, held on the monopod](11-dji-scanning-height.jpg)

### Environmental best practices

- **Height discipline:** do not raise or lower the camera during an active pass. Keep the 1.6 m elevation exactly.
- **Wall clearance:** keep at least one arm's length of clearance from walls and structural barriers.
- **Lens clearance:** make sure that all lenses are clean and are not obstructed by straps, mounts, or cables.
- **Pacing:** keep a steady walking speed of approximately 1 m/s through both passes. Do not make sudden movements or fast rotations.
- **Grid spacing:** keep the parallel grid lines evenly spaced to prevent coverage gaps in the area of interest.
- **Obstacle navigation:** if an obstacle blocks a grid line, go around it smoothly, keep the camera orientation and height, then continue the line immediately after you pass.

### Step-by-step operational workflow

**Core principle:** recording stays **continuous** from start to finish. Do not start and stop the recording between nodes.

**Scanning height:** hold the DJI 360 camera on the monopod at **1.6 meters (5 feet 3 inches)** above ground level. Keep the camera level through the pass. Give the camera the maximum view and keep it unobstructed.

#### Step 1: perimeter scan

- Start at a designated corner of the area of interest (AOI).
- Walk continuously along the outer boundary of the AOI until the perimeter is fully enclosed.

![Perimeter scanning route: walk continuously along the outer boundary until the perimeter is enclosed](11-dji-route-1-perimeter.png)

#### Step 2: X-axis grid traverse

- Move to the starting boundary of the X-axis.
- Walk forth and back along the X-axis, left to right then right to left, in parallel lines until you traverse the full length of the area.

![X-axis scanning route: parallel lines left to right and right to left across the length of the area](11-dji-route-2-x-axis.png)

#### Step 3: Y-axis grid traverse

- Pivot 90 degrees to align with the Y-axis.
- Walk forth and back along the Y-axis, top to bottom then bottom to top, in parallel lines across the full width of the area.

![Y-axis scanning route: parallel lines top to bottom and bottom to top across the width of the area](11-dji-route-3-y-axis.png)

#### Step 4: completion

Stop the recording when you return to the final point. Power down the equipment with the standard shutdown procedure.

## 4. Post-capture and power down

1. Stop the continuous recording from the DJI MIMO app or the camera button.
2. Press and hold the **Power** button until the unit powers off.
3. Secure the camera hardware and store the media cards for ingestion into the Polytron.AI engine.
