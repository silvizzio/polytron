The Notifications tab shows all the alert events. To acknowledge them, click **View**, or use the notifications panel on the left.

![The notifications list with the alert events](06-notifications-list.png)

Select the alert type and enter the remarks before you acknowledge the alert.

![Select the alert type and enter remarks](06-notifications-acknowledge.png)
