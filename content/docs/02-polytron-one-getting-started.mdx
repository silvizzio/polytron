## About POLYTRON ONE

POLYTRON ONE brings camera management, live video, AI event detection, alarms, and playback into one platform. It also provides tools for managing users and system settings.

The platform connects video feeds with AI analytics to monitor people, vehicles, areas, behavior, and equipment status. When it detects an abnormal event, it can trigger an alarm and link the alert to live images, recorded clips, and event details. Users can then review the event and trace it back to the relevant footage.

This guide is for POLYTRON ONE users. It follows the common workflows: live view, camera management, playback, notifications, and system configuration.

## Login

Enter the IP address, email address, and password. Select **Remember Me** if you want the platform to remember your login.

## Home page and overview panel

After you log in, the Overview, Alerts, and Notifications panels are on the left.

- The **Overview** panel shows the total number of cameras in the system, how many are online and offline, and the CPU, RAM, and disk usage.
- The **Alerts** panel shows the total number of violations, and how many are pending and resolved.
- The **Notifications** panel gives a quick view of the recent notifications.

To minimize or restore the panel, click the three bars at the top.
