This site holds the field procedures and the platform user guide for POLYTRON.AI.

## Scanning field guides

Spatial capture gives the core imagery that the POLYTRON AI platform needs to build high-fidelity digital twins, 3D maps, and immersive virtual tours. These assets drive operational awareness, let teams inspect remotely, and give precise visualisation.

Four guides cover four site conditions. Choose by camera and by output.

| Guide | Camera | Mount | Height | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Indoor Congested Area](/polytron/docs/08-indoor-congested-area) | Insta360 360 | Tripod | 1.6 m | Virtual tour |
| [Indoor Area](/polytron/docs/09-indoor-area) | Insta360 360 | Tripod | 1.8 m | Virtual tour |
| [Outdoor Area](/polytron/docs/10-outdoor-area) | Insta360 360 | Monopod | 1.8 m | Virtual tour |
| [3D Reconstruction and Mapping](/polytron/docs/11-3d-reconstruction-mapping) | DJI 360 | Monopod | 1.6 m | 3D mesh and map |

The three virtual tour guides use the same walk-pause capture cycle. The distances between pause points and the mounting height change with the site. The DJI guide uses a different pattern: a perimeter pass, then a two-axis grid traverse.

## POLYTRON ONE user guide

[Polytron One](/polytron/docs/02-polytron-one-getting-started) is the platform where the captured footage and the live camera feeds are managed. The user guide covers camera management, live view, playback, notifications, and system settings.

## Before you go to site

- Confirm the camera, the mount, and the height for your site condition from the table above.
- Charge the batteries and confirm at least 128 GB of free memory.
- Clean all lenses.
- Read the full guide for your condition. Do the field quality checks before you leave the site.
