Live View lets you see cameras in multiple panels.

Click **Add New View** to select a multi-panel viewing mode. Click **Rename** to rename the view. Click **+** on a blank screen to add a camera to that panel.

![Add a new view and select the panel layout](04-live-view-add.png)

After you click **Add Camera**, the camera list appears on the left. Select the cameras that you want to add to the screen. To delete a view, click the **X** next to its name.

![Select cameras from the list to fill the panels](04-live-view-cameras.png)
