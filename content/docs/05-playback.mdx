There are two ways to find and play back video: behavior search, or manual date and time search.

## Behavior search

Select the start date and time and the end date and time for the search. Enter the behavior that you want to find. The results appear. Select a video from the results to play it.

![The behavior search panel with the results list](05-behavior-search.png)

## Manual search

Before you start a manual search, clear all the previous search results. Set the date and time of the search, select the camera from the camera list, then search.

![The manual search panel](05-manual-search.png)

After you select a camera, click the calendar to choose a date. A red dot marks the days that have recordings. The time bar below shows when recordings are available for the selected camera. Click the time bar to start playback. To expand the time bar, scroll with the mouse wheel.

![The playback timeline, with the recording availability bar](05-manual-search-timeline.png)

## Export video

After you watch the video, click the **scissors** button to trim it. Use the timeline to select the part that you want to export, then click **Export**. The video file downloads to the Downloads folder on your computer.

![Trim the clip on the timeline, then export it](05-export-video.png)
