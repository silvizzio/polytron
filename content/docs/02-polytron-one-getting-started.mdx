## About POLYTRON ONE

POLYTRON ONE is an integrated platform for smart security and intelligent video management. It supports camera access management, real-time video preview, AI event detection, alarm linkage processing, video playback and retrieval, and system configuration management.

The platform combines unified access to multiple video resources with AI video analytics. This lets it identify and monitor people, vehicles, areas, behaviors, and equipment status continuously. When it detects an abnormal event, it can trigger an alarm automatically and link the real-time images, the notification center alerts, the video clips, and the event details. This gives a closed loop from video access, to intelligent detection, to alarm triggering, to event handling, to video traceability.

This guide is for POLYTRON ONE users. It follows the common workflows: live view, camera management, playback, notifications, and system configuration.

## Login

Enter the IP address, the email, and the password. Before you log in, you can select or clear the **Remember Me** checkbox.

## Home page and overview panel

After you log in, the Overview, Alerts, and Notifications panels are on the left.

- The **Overview** panel shows the total number of cameras in the system, how many are online and offline, and the CPU, RAM, and disk usage.
- The **Alerts** panel shows the total number of violations, and how many are pending and resolved.
- The **Notifications** panel gives a quick view of the recent notifications.

To minimize or restore the panel, click the three bars at the top.
