Operational instructions for live 3D mapping and virtual tour creation.

## 1. Executive summary and purpose

Your spatial capture workflow gives the core imagery that is necessary to generate high-fidelity digital twins, 3D maps, and immersive virtual tours in the **Polytron.AI** platform. These assets drive operational awareness, let teams inspect remotely, and give precise visualisation.

## 2. Hardware requirements and pre-recording setup

### Essential hardware checklist

- **Insta360 360 camera:** the primary capture device for panoramic spherical imagery.
- **Tripod:** a rigid, stable mount.
- **Power and memory:** spare batteries for extended operations, and a minimum of **128 GB memory storage**.
- **Mobile control device:** a smartphone or tablet that runs the Insta360 app, for remote monitoring and capture control.

### Camera configuration setup

1. **Power on:** press the **Power** button once to turn on the camera.
2. **Video settings:** set the recording resolution to **5.7K at 30 FPS**.
3. **App pair (optional):** enable Bluetooth and Wi-Fi on your mobile device to connect to the Insta360 app for remote start and stop.
4. **Mounting height:** for indoor congested areas with static capture at all points, mount the camera at **1.6 meters (5 feet 3 inches)** above ground level. Make sure that the rig is stable and level.

### Environmental best practices

**Height consistency:** keep the 1.6 meter mounting height for the full capture session.

**Wall clearance:** keep at least one arm's length of clearance from walls and structural barriers.

**Lens clearance:** make sure that all lenses are clean and are not obstructed by straps, mounts, or cables.

**Doorway transitions:** pause before you enter a doorway, walk through while the camera records continuously, then pause again after you exit.

**Pacing:** walk at a steady pace of approximately 1 meter per second to prevent motion blur and drift.

## 3. Standardized operational workflow

### Operational sequence

**Core principle:** recording stays **continuous** from start to finish. Do not start and stop the recording between nodes.

| Phase | Action | Movement / Distance | Duration / Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Start** | Initial static capture | Stationary | Put the camera tripod on the floor, start the recording, and move away so that the camera stays still for **15 seconds**. This lets the internal gyros settle. |
| **2. Walk** | Continuous move | **2 to 3 m** | Hold the camera slightly above 1.8 meters and walk forward at a steady pace of approximately 1 m/s. Keep line of sight to the previous pause point. |
| **3. Pause** | Static anchor capture | Stationary | Put the camera tripod on the floor and move away so that the camera stays stationary for **15 seconds** while it records. |
| **4. Loop** | Repeat sequence | Walk, pause cycle | Repeat the walk-pause cycle across the full site until you have complete area coverage. |
| **5. End** | Final static capture | Stationary | Put the camera tripod on the floor and move away so that the camera stays stationary for **15 seconds** while it records, then stop the recording. |

## 4. Key anchor locations and spacing guidelines

### Critical pause points

Always do a 15-second stationary pause at these locations:

- **Entrances and exits:** immediately before and after you cross any doorway threshold.
- **Corners and turns:** before you turn a corner, and immediately after you complete the turn.
- **Intersections:** at the center of hall or path intersections.
- **Level transitions:** at the top and the bottom of staircases or ramps.
- **Complex assets:** in front of key landmarks, CCTV installations, control panels, or high-value equipment.

### Field operator quick reference rules

**Rule of thumb 1: walk, pause, look back.** After you move, pause for 15 seconds and confirm visually that you can still see the previous pause location. Sufficient overlap gives seamless 3D reconstruction.

**Rule of thumb 2: threshold and corner buffers.** Always pause directly before and directly after you go through doorways or blind corners.

**Rule of thumb 3: if in doubt, pause.** If continuous visual coverage feels interrupted or blocked, add an extra 15-second pause point before you continue.

## 5. Post-capture and power down

1. Confirm that the final 15-second static capture is complete.
2. Stop the continuous recording from the app or the camera button.
3. Press and hold the **Power** button until the unit powers off.
4. Secure the camera hardware and store the media cards for ingestion into the Polytron.AI engine.
